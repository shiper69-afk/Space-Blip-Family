# Grandma Chaya call script (full, from Shiper)
NOTE: Title card in game must be "Are We There Yet? (Galaxy edition)" / "Episode 1: Grandma's Calling" (not SPACE BLIP FAMILY).
NOTE: No 20:00 / 19:59 countdown and no Ship Computer “Grandma will call back in twenty minutes.” Grandma calling back is a later beat.
NOTE: Walk-ins are empty-handed (no coffee / manual / frying pan graphics). Stage directions about holding those stay in this script; they are not staged. Spoken lines that mention a prop (e.g. “put that book down”) stay as dialogue.

INT. COCKPIT, CONTINUOUS

*BOOP. The main screen fills with GRANDMA CHAYA'S face, much too close to her camera. All we see are huge reading glasses and one eyebrow.*

CHAYA: Hello? HELLO? Is this thing on? Naomi, your father set this up, and I don't trust it.

*She leans back. Her living room is spotless: plastic covers on the furniture, a cat sitting perfectly upright.*

DAVDAV (waving with both hands): Hi Grandma!
CHAYA: DavDav! My baby! (squints) Is that jam on your face?
DAVDAV: It's space jam.
CHAYA: Of course it is. Where is everybody? I haven't seen you children in... (counts on her fingers, runs out) ...forever.

*ALAN snores in the captain's chair, a coffee mug rising and falling on his belly.*

CHAYA: Is that the Captain? ASLEEP at the wheel?
DAVDAV: He's resting his eyes.
CHAYA: He's been resting his eyes since the wedding. ALAN!

*Alan jolts awake. The mug flies and sticks to the console.*

ALAN: I'M AWAKE! I'M DRIVING! (sees the screen, freezes) ...Chaya. What a wonderful surprise.
CHAYA: Is it, Alan?
ALAN: Every call is a gift.

*NAOMI walks in holding her coffee. She sees the screen and stops cold.*

NAOMI: Hi, Mom.
CHAYA: Naomi, sweetheart. You look tired.
NAOMI: It's six in the morning.
CHAYA: I was up at four. I've cleaned the whole house twice. Where are my other grandchildren? Or have they forgotten they have a grandmother?
ALAN (over intercom): Kids! Bridge! Grandma's on the line!

*Thundering footsteps. JOE runs in first, half asleep.*

JOE: Who authorized an outside call? This is a breach of protocol!
CHAYA: Joseph. "Protocol." You used to cry when the toaster popped.
JOE: ...That toaster was a threat.

*DANDAN shuffles in holding a frying pan.*

DANDAN: Hi, Grandma. Did you eat? I'm working on a nebula omelet.
CHAYA: DanDan, you're too thin. Are they feeding you?
DANDAN: I'm the one who feeds THEM.
CHAYA: (to Naomi, sighing) The thirteen-year-old does the cooking.
NAOMI: He likes cooking.
CHAYA: I didn't say anything.
NAOMI: You said it with your eyebrow.

*EITHAN walks in last, reading a ship manual, not looking up.*

EITHAN: Status update. Call quality is sixty-two percent. Grandma's camera is pointed at the ceiling.
CHAYA: It is not. (tilts the camera, now showing only her chin) Eithan, put that book down and look at your grandmother.
EITHAN: (lowers it two inches) Hi, Grandma.
CHAYA: Look at all of you. Pale. Skinny. Half asleep at six in the morning.
ALAN: That's... a normal time to be asleep.

*Chaya's eyes drift past the family to the mess behind them: socks hanging on the nav screen, a half-eaten sandwich stuck to the ceiling, a laundry pile with a blinking light inside.*

CHAYA: Alan. Is that a SOCK on the navigation screen?
ALAN: It cuts the glare.
CHAYA: And the sandwich on the ceiling?
DAVDAV: That's from Thursday!
JOE: It's evidence. Nobody touch it.
CHAYA: (closes her eyes, deep breath) Your grandfather flew a cargo hauler for forty years. You could eat off his dashboard. You could do surgery on his floor.
DANDAN: You can eat off our ceiling.

*Naomi elbows him.*

CHAYA: Now you listen to me, all of you. Grandma is going to hang up. Grandma is going to make herself a nice cup of tea. And in twenty minutes, Grandma is calling back. When I do, I want to see a clean ship, a hot breakfast, and every one of you awake, combed, and SMILING.
ALAN: Twenty minutes. No problem. Easy. Piece of cake.
DANDAN: We don't have cake.
CHAYA: And Alan.
ALAN: Yes, Chaya?
CHAYA: Comb your hair. What's left of it.

*She jabs at her screen.*

CHAYA: How do I hang up? Where's the... Frank! How do I hang up this thing? FRAAANK?

*Her face freezes mid-shout. "CALL ENDED."*

(Production: no 20:00 countdown, no Ship Computer VO. Grandma calling back is a later beat.)

*Every alarm goes off. Amber lights flash. Silence on the bridge. The whole family turns slowly to look at DavDav.*

DAVDAV: ...Red means yes.
NAOMI: Okay. Joe, lock down the laundry. DanDan, breakfast. Eithan, figure out why the synthesizer sounds like it's dying. Alan—
ALAN (heroic pose): Fear not, crew! Your Captain has everything under control!

*He steps forward and trips over a toy rocket.*

NARRATOR (V.O.): He does not have everything under control.

*Freeze frame on Alan mid-fall.*

TITLE CARD: Are We There Yet? (Galaxy edition)
Subtitle: "Episode 1: Grandma's Calling"

*Fade back in. The family scatters. The player takes control of Alan.*
